Balans is a solo data and AI engineering practice. I build local, in-process, on-device solutions that keep your data private and under your control, designed around FAIR data principles, and WCAG 2.2 / EN 301 549, without vendor lock-in or long-term contracts.

# Why a Different Approach

Digital modernization is often sold as a platform, a subscription, a dashboard, or a multi-year framework contract that outlives the mandate it was built for. European institutions are also asking harder questions about where their data lives, who can access it, and how easily they can leave a vendor. I work differently.

## Embedded, Scoped, Handed Over

I work with your team, within your constraints, until the deliverable is complete and handed over. After that, you decide whether to extend the engagement or close it.

**Built for public-sector reality:**

- **Local-first, sovereign by default**: data and models run on infrastructure you control, with no third-party cloud routing unless you explicitly choose it.
- **No vendor lock-in**: deliverables include complete code, documentation, and training.
- **Open-source stack**: tools you can inspect, host yourselves, and hand off without a license fee.
- **Project-based**: defined scope, defined end date, sized to fit standard public procurement processes.
- **Standards-aligned from day one**: built to WCAG 2.2 AA (AAA criteria where feasible) and EN 301 549, with data structured to FAIR principles.

# What I Deliver

Fix the data, make it actionable, then add advanced capabilities, with privacy, accessibility, and sovereignty as underlying principles.

## Data Engineering

Custom pipelines and analytical infrastructure that turn raw data into reusable assets. Suited to organizations consolidating legacy systems, opening up datasets, or preparing for AI-enabled initiatives.

## Reporting & Analytical Deliverables

Reports, notebooks, dashboards, and AI layers that draw on your project's data and your team's knowledge, published in accessible formats.

## AI/ML Engineering

From proof of concept to working deployment, with transparency and fairness at the core. I specialize in on-device inference, so sensitive data never has to leave your environment.

## Strategy, Governance & Compliance

I help align your data with organizational goals and with regulatory and standards requirements (national access-to-information rules, the EU AI Act, WCAG, FAIR), so you go into internal or external review with your documentation in order.

## Sovereign Tooling

Local, in-process, on-device inference and tooling by default, supporting data sovereignty, privacy, and full auditability.
